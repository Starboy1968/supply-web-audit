# 01_scope: fix the boundary

One job: say what is inside this audit, who it serves, and what is left out.

## Inputs
- Working (this run): client brief or owner notes (supplied by the person)
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/client-profile.md
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/layers-and-tiers.md

Do NOT load: other stages, `principles.md`, any client records yet.

## Process
1. Name the client, the decision, and the beneficiary (from the profile).
2. Pick the layers in play and declare the boundary cut.
3. List what is not known (gaps). Max one page.

## Outputs
- scope.md → output/

## Human check
Read scope.md aloud and confirm the boundary is right. Edit in place; the next stage reads whatever is here.
