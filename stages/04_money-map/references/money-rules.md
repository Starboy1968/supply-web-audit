# Money rules
- Hydra rule: finance is many heads. Count the heads the client depends on.
- Capital is a lever, not a crown. Falsifier: a player that wins with capital now but loses its options later.
- 30% test: cut credit and asset values 30%. Count months until options are gone.
- Shared-parent check: two lenders with the same parent, bank, or fund are one head.
- Buffer days: cash on hand divided by daily outflow.
