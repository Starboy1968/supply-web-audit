# AI use review
Roles: tool, player, infrastructure, shock generator.
Autonomy ladder: 0 none, 1 suggests, 2 drafts, 3 acts with approval, 4 acts alone.
Use modes: draft, recommend, act, check, explore.
Multiplier test: AI scales whatever it is attached to. Bad input, bad output, faster.
Constraint rule: AI moves the bottleneck to input quality, judgment, verification. A human gate counts only if it has time, information, authority, and no penalty for saying no.

Eight questions before AI enters a process:
1. Task type (create or decide)?
2. Who is stronger here, human or AI?
3. Where is the bottleneck?
4. How good is the input?
5. What are the stakes?
6. Who is the gate, and can they say no?
7. What does it depend on (vendor, model, cloud)?
8. What is the exit if it fails?

Evidence: Vaccaro et al. 2024 meta-analysis (106 experiments): human-AI combinations lost on average (g = -0.23), with losses when AI was the stronger party. Interaction style was not isolated.
