# Stage 1: Scope
Job: Fix what is inside the audit and why.

## Inputs
Working (this run):
- Client brief or notes from the owner
Reference (every run):
- `_shared/layers-and-tiers.md`
- `_shared/principles.md`

## Process
1. Name the client, the product or service, and the decision the audit supports.
2. Name the beneficiary.
3. Pick the layers in play. Declare the boundary cut.
4. List what you do not know (gaps).

## Outputs
- `01-scope/output/scope.md` ending with `Human check: passed`

## Human check
Owner reads scope.md and confirms the boundary is right. Say 'confirmed' or what to change.
