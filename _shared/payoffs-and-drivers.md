# Payoffs and drivers
Five payoffs (they conflict; read each player's trade-off): self-preservation, freedom, leverage, options, relationships. Open question: is certainty a sixth? (Gap)

Four drivers: greed, impulse control, fear, pride. Score process, not outcome. Greed and impulse control separate useful capital from dumb capital.
Rich is not wealthy. Wealthy means compounding assets, low fragile debt, buffers, options, stable rules.
