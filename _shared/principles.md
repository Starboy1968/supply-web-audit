# Seven core principles
1. **Links**: the web is what is connected. The real link is often not on the formal map.
2. **Leverage**: sits with whoever can walk away more cheaply.
3. **Capacity**: slack, buffers, options. Slack is the survival variable.
4. **Fragility**: narrow points, shared dependencies, keystones, loops, thresholds, delays.
5. **Behavior**: players act on payoffs and drivers. Read what they do, not what they say.
6. **Time**: obligations carry shocks forward. Weeks to recover matters more than size of loss.
7. **Honesty**: label evidence, state alternatives, name gaps. Applies to this framework too.

Boundary rule: a finding belongs to the principle that explains it best. Do not count it twice.
