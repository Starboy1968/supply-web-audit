# Seven layers and three tiers
Layers (all connected): money, physical, rules, narrative, technology, behavior, ecology. Rank links by strength and speed. Declare the boundary cut: what was left out and why.

Tiers: top, middle, bottom. Set per link, not per player. Credit and losses flow down; labor and consumption flow up. The middle is the swing tier.
Beneficiary: working classes and the SMBs between them and capital.

Roster admission test: add a player only if removing it changes another player's payoffs or options.
Extra roster types: chokepoint holders, insurers, trust gatekeepers, platforms, information shapers, security forces, shadow players, foreign states, AI agents, collectives, fixed-asset owners, absent entrants.
Weather, disease, geology are shock generators, not players.
