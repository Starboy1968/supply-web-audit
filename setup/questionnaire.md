# Setup questionnaire
Ask the owner these once per client copy. Write the answers to `_shared/client-profile.md`.
Shortcut: if the auditor already filled `tools/intake-form.html` and saved `intake/client-profile.md`, read it back to the owner, confirm each answer, then copy it to `_shared/client-profile.md`.

1. Client name and what they sell or do.
2. The decision this audit supports (and the date it is due).
3. Who is the beneficiary of the audit?
4. Which layers matter most (money, physical, rules, narrative, technology, behavior, ecology)?
5. Which AI tools does the client use, and who approves their output?
6. What records can the client give (bank, loans, AR/AP aging, contracts, two periods of payment history)?
7. Risk appetite: what loss or delay would the owner not accept? (open design question)
8. Is legal review done for scoring any named person's behavior? Default: score firms only until it is.
9. Who else reads the report, and what must they find without asking?
